I want to write, but I feel no spark,  
Like my magic was stolen and locked in the dark.  
I dive so deep just to end up ashore,  
No matter how far,  
I’m pushed back once more.  

Are waves guiding me, pulling my strings,  
Or are illusions just dressed up as shiny things?  
I search for my feelings in the grains of sand,  
Finding shells in my hands was not what I planned.  

I dive back again,  
With a reason new  
Now I look for a pearl,  
Not the ache,  
Not the loss,  
And it shines in my hand like it knew its cost.  

I wanna write of its light, keep it close, keep it true,  
Hide it from the world, or give it to you.  
Now I see why the shore pulled me free  
Cause some things aren’t lost.  
They’re just found differently.
