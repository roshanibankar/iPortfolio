## A list of qualities I look for in Love [Romantic & Platonic & Self]

- The goal is to be known not just chosen

- Shared curiosity and laughter rather than shared interests every-time

- accepting the Double-Edged Sword Risk [Opening yourself to meaningful connections also opens you to disappointment and heartbreak. They're a package deal. Don't let the possibility of losing people make you unwilling to welcome them.]

- Share my Weight, But Never Outsource my Gravity [i wanna be Independent at my own core but at the same time trust them, give them the benefit of doubt, try to understand "why" before jumping to conclusions.]

- Legacy, aging, decay [Let yourself enjoy being someone's friend without constantly worrying about whether you're still their friend. People evolve, change their minds and surprise you. Treat people like unfinished artwork cause never assume you've figured someone out completelyLeave room to keep discovering the people you love rather than holding them hostage to an older version of themselves.]

- Accountability Is the Highest Form of Gentleness [The ability to say "I made a mistake and I'm sorry" without shifting blame or invalidating someone's feelings is essential to every meaningful relationship. Accountability is how trust gets repaired.]

- Grand Gestures Must Honor the Quiet Days [i've got a romantic example but the baseline is the same for friendships too- People generally think grand gestures are performative and are often dismissed as being hollow. But imagine this scenario:
On a weekend, he doesn't just take me out; he organizes a private, after-hours tour of a landmark building I’ve been dying to see, completely cleared out just for the two of us. Inside, he has set up a small table with the exact street food we always grab on a random Tuesday and then he hands me a rare, out-of-print architecture book I mentioned in passing months ago. Seems like he’s McDreamy, right?
And don’t get me wrong, a woman can do these gestures too, in the ways he’d love.
An authentic grand gesture is never a loud distraction or a generic stunt. It is an explosive, vibrant new stroke painted across your daily routine. It takes familiar pieces of your life and elevates them; it doesn't try to replace or make up for the quiet Tuesdays, it honors them.]

- Love Is a Choice on a Random Tuesday
What would a Tuesday afternoon look like with them?
So if I had to write about my random Tuesday, it might look like this:
I spend the day working on architectural renders. In the evening, I get a message from him:
"Found a book I think you'd like."
We meet up.
Maybe we grab food, walk around, talk about some building, some movie or some ridiculous friendship dilemma.
At some point, there's a hug, maybe a hand held absent-mindedly.
Nothing dramatic happens.
And I go home feeling:
"My life is better because this person is in it."
Ironically, that's not very cinematic.
But most lasting love stories are built from a lot of Tuesdays, not from one perfect kiss where the leg pops.
Same goes for catching up with friends, calling them for no reason to sometimes just to unwind if they feel like it too.

- Knowing How I Give and Receive Love [making space to communicate how I feel loved without expecting everyone to love exactly as I do, boils down to mutual willingness and consideration in both directions rather than sameness]
